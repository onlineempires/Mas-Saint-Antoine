Must-Visit Attractions

From UNESCO World Heritage sites to hidden gems known only to locals, discover the treasures that make Provence unforgettable.

Avignon - City of Popes

UNESCO Site

Explore the magnificent Palais des Papes, one of the largest Gothic palaces in the world. Stroll through medieval streets, cross the famous Pont d'Avignon, and immerse yourself in a city that was once the center of Christian Europe.

Palais des Papes (Palace of the Popes)
Pont Saint-Bénézet (Pont d'Avignon)
Avignon Theatre Festival (July)
Les Halles covered market
