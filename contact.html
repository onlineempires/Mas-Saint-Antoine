Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers to common questions about Mas Saint Antoine and your stay.

Check-in is from 4:00 PM onwards, and check-out is before 11:00 AM. We offer flexible arrival times with advance notice. Our team will meet you personally to show you around the property and your gîte.

The pool is heated during the season (typically April through October) and is available 24/7 for guests. We maintain the temperature at a comfortable 26-28°C (79-82°F).

Yes, we offer shuttle service to/from airports and train stations. This service must be booked in advance and charges apply based on distance. We can also recommend reliable taxi services.

We welcome well-behaved pets in certain gîtes with advance notice. A small additional fee applies, and we ask that pets be kept on leash in common areas and cleaned up after.

Our welcome pack includes fresh bread, local olive oil, a selection of Provençal herbs, regional wine, coffee, tea, and basic pantry items. We can customize this based on dietary requirements with advance notice.
